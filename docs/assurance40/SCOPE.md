# Scope reconciliation — W02

## Assurance campaign navigation

The [L1 surface declaration](../BETA-LANGUAGE.md) is the public boundary.
The [architectural ADR](../ADR-AXIAL-0001.md) is a revised proposal, not an
activation or amendment of that boundary. The [claim index](../CLAIMS.md)
distinguishes L1, standalone CLI and opt-in host/consumer research. The
[release gate](../RELEASE-GATE.md) specifies candidate-specific evidence.

The [campaign ledger](STATUS.json) records implementation and verification
separately. Audit40/Audit80 and A00–A19 records remain historical unless
rerun for a named candidate. No native target, universal mutation mediation,
confidential receipt or external relying-party qualification follows from
the campaign. PR/non-main evidence excludes elevated trusted-main authority
qualification. No public ABI or runtime change is implied.
